# SST- 1. Skilled Traders 🔥🔥🔥
Skilled traders are needed around the world. These jobs are good for those who are not good at the office and are paid more than the average pay in Canada.  

# SST- 2. Phone Interviews 🔥🔥🔥
Interviewers cannot see your face in a phone interview. It is very important to be confident. You need to give a short pause and listen to the questions.  

# SST- 3. Orientation 🔥🔥
Orientations are training for new employees in an organization. Some orientations are boring and a waste of time. Orientations with small sessions will be more effective.  

# SST- 4. The First Day of School 🔥🔥🔥
Children should remember the names of the teachers and the numbers of the classrooms on the first day of school. Don't forget to prepare money to buy lunch.  

# SST- 5. Job Interviews 🔥🔥🔥
What the interviewer is seeking in a job interview is your thoughts and your story, not just answers to the questions. During the interview, the recruiter is evaluating your abilities.  

# SST- 6. Cleaning the Park 🔥🔥🔥
The speaker announced teams for park cleaning. There are three jobs about cleaning the park: cleaning the trash, checking things that shouldn't be there, repainting the wall.

# SST- 7. Wash Fruits and Vegetables 🔥🔥🔥
We should use cold water to wash the outside of the fruits and vegetables if they have peels. Read the label if they are packaged.  

# SST- 8. Railpass 🔥🔥🔥
Railpass is a train transit card that gives you access not only to the network of trains, but also to discounts. You need to do more planning before traveling.
