Write Email 5-Step Memorization Trainer 2.3

Email Library (.txt)

* Use "#" at the start of a line for IDs and Titles.

Step 1: Controlled Reading

Read the full email once only. Do not memorize details.
Focus on:
  • What each paragraph does
  • How each paragraph begins
Paste or Load the full original text below.

Step 2: Structure Encoding

Without looking back at the original text:
  • What does the opening paragraph do?
  • How many main points are in the body?
  • What does the closing paragraph do?
Use notes or short phrases.

Step 3: Sentence Starters

Write the first sentence (or sentence starter) of each paragraph.
Example:
  • I am writing to...
  • Firstly, about...
  • It would be great if...

Step 4: Guided Recall

Without seeing the original text:
  • Use the structure and sentence starters
  • Write the complete email
  • This is not timed

Step 5: Full Recall

Write the complete email from memory.
  • No original text
  • Timed task (9 minutes)
  • You may pause if needed
